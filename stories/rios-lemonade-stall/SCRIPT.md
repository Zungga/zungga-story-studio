# Rio's Lemonade Stall — pilot episode 1 (draft for review)

- **For:** Class 3–5 (Tamil Nadu + Karnataka state boards), English.
- **Topic:** Maths, money: adding prices, money spent vs money earned, profit and loss.
- **Length:** about 4 minutes, vertical 1080 × 1920.
- **Cast:** Narrator, Rio, Tara, Mira, Customer uncle. Each has their own voice from voice casting.
- **Place:** a colony street on a hot summer day, with Rio's stall and the park across the road.

## The three pauses (why every kid can answer)
1. **Look** (scene 12): the answer is on screen, the crowd walking to the park. Anyone can say it, which builds confidence.
2. **Choose** (scene 14): three picture cards; kids show 1, 2 or 3 with their fingers. It works for shy kids and for a whole class at once.
3. **Count** (scene 18): Class 3–5 syllabus maths, counting in tens to 70, then adding 50.
Each answer is confirmed and praised right after ("If you said two…"), so kids feel they got it right.

## Missions (scene 22)
- Three unrelated choices at the same effort: movement, family, creative.
- Worded as "a grown-up at home", never "mom".
