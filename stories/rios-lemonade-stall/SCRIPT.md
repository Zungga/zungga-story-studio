# Rio's Lemonade Stall — pilot episode 1 (draft for review)

- **For:** Class 3–5 (Tamil Nadu + Karnataka state boards), English.
- **Topic:** Maths, money: adding prices, money spent vs money earned, profit and loss.
- **Length:** about 3½ minutes, vertical 1080 × 1920.
- **Cast:** Narrator, Rio, Tara, Mira, Customer uncle. Each has their own voice from voice casting.
- **Place:** a colony street on a hot summer day, with Rio's stall and the park across the road.

| # | Who | Line | On screen |
|---|-----|------|-----------|
| 1 | Narrator | It was the hottest day of the whole summer. The sun was shining, the roads were sizzling, and everyone in the colony was thirsty. That is when Rio had a big idea. | Heat shimmer over the street, then Rio pops up with a lemon in each hand. |
| 2 | Rio | Lemonade! Ice-cold lemonade! I will open the coolest shop in the whole colony! | Rio jumps; a "RIO'S COOL LEMONADE" sign flips up. |
| 3 | Tara | A shop needs things to sell, Rio. What do we have to buy first? | Tara opens her notebook. |
| 4 | Narrator | So they went shopping. Lemons cost thirty rupees. Sugar cost twenty rupees. And paper cups cost twenty rupees. | Three price cards drop in: ₹30, ₹20, ₹20. |
| 5 | Tara | Thirty plus twenty plus twenty. We spent seventy rupees. | The notebook shows 30 + 20 + 20 = **₹70 spent**. |
| 6 | Rio | No problem! We have twelve cups of lemonade. Ten rupees a cup. We will be rich! | 12 cups line up on the table; a "₹10 a cup" tag. |
| 7 | Uncle | Arre, lemonade on such a hot day? Wonderful! One cup for me, young man. | The uncle hands Rio a ₹10 note; *ka-ching*. |
| 8 | Narrator | One by one, five people bought a cup. Fifty rupees went into Rio's box. | The coin-box counter goes 10 → 50. |
| 9 | Narrator | But then, nobody came. The sun got hotter, the ice melted, and the lemonade turned warm. | The street empties; ice cubes shrink. |
| 10 | Mira | Rio… why is everybody walking to the park? | People walk past toward the park across the road. |
| 11 | Rio | Wait. We spent seventy rupees, and we only got fifty. Did we… lose money?! | Rio's face drops; 70 vs 50 appears in red. |
| 12 | Tara | Seventy minus fifty is twenty. We lost twenty rupees. | **Loss: ₹20** stamps on the notebook. |
| 13 | Rio (to camera) | Friends, I need YOUR help! I still have seven cups left. How can I stop losing money? Pause the video, think hard, and tell your idea out loud! | **THE BIG PAUSE:** a thinking bubble and a 10-second timer. Clues glow: 7 cups left · it is very hot · everyone is going to the park. |
| 14 | Narrator | Did you get an idea? Let's see what Rio's friends thought! | The timer pops. |
| 15 | Mira | Ooh, I know! Let's make a giant sign with a smiling lemon on it, so everyone can see it from far away! | Mira paints the big lemon sign. |
| 16 | Tara | And let's move the stall next to the park gate. That is where all the people are! | The stall rolls to the park gate. |
| 17 | Narrator | They added fresh ice, put up the big sign, and moved to the park. And then… | Fresh ice clinks; a crowd turns around. |
| 18 | Uncle | Two cups please! My grandchildren are very thirsty! | Quick sales: 7 cups gone, counter goes 50 → 120. |
| 19 | Tara | We got one hundred and twenty rupees. We spent seventy. One hundred and twenty minus seventy is fifty. We made fifty rupees of profit! | **Profit = Money got − Money spent** · 120 − 70 = **₹50**. |
| 20 | Rio | Profit! Some of you said move to where the people are, and some of you said make a big sign. Both are super ideas. Well done, business brains! | Confetti; a "Business Brain" badge. |
| 21 | Narrator | Remember: before you sell, count what you spend. Then sell where your customers are! | Lesson card. |
| 22 | Rio | Now it is YOUR turn! Pick one mission, do it in real life, and ask a grown-up at home to approve it. | **Missions, choose one:** ① Do 10 lemon-squeeze jumps (jump and squeeze your hands) · ② Help a grown-up put away the shopping and find 3 price tags · ③ Draw your own shop sign with a price on it. |
| 23 | Narrator | Next time, a new lemonade stall opens right across the road, selling lemonade for only five rupees! What will Rio do? | The cliffhanger: a rival stall with a "₹5!" sign. |

## The idea moment (scene 13)
- One clear problem, with numbers a Class 3 child can handle (70, 50, 20).
- Three visible clues, so kids feel they *can* solve it.
- Several right answers (move, sign, ice, lower price…). Scene 20 praises the common ones.

## Missions (scene 22)
- Three unrelated choices at the same effort: movement, family, creative.
- Worded as "a grown-up at home", never "mom".
